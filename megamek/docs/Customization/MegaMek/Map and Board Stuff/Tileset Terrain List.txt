MEGAMEK TILESET - TERRAIN LIST
==============================================================================
Source: megamek/common/units/Terrains.java (names[] array).
The keyword is what the tileset uses to match a hex to an image.
"Level" = the number after the keyword (e.g. woods:2 = heavy woods).

Grouped by what it means for the artist. Everything under "NEEDS NO ART" is
bookkeeping - numbers the engine reads or edges it draws itself.


==============================================================================

==============================================================================
  woods              1 light, 2 heavy, 3 ultra
  jungle             1 light, 2 heavy, 3 ultra
  water              level = depth (also edge-connected, see below)
  rough              1 normal, 2 ultra
  rubble             1 light .. 4 hardened, 5 wall, 6 ultra
  sand               -
  tundra             -

  swamp              1 normal, 2/3 quicksand
