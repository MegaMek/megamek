MEGAMEK DEFAULT KEY BINDS
=========================

Every bind listed here can be changed in Settings (Alt+C) on the Key Binds tab.
Names match the labels shown on that tab.

Some keys appear more than once. That is intentional: binds that never apply in
the same phase can share a key, e.g. Punch (P) and Go Prone (P). Binds that are
marked [menu] belong to the menu bar and cannot share their key with anything.

Needs the TacOps called shots game option. Sets the called shot for the weapon
selected in the unit display. Press the same direction again to clear it.
High and Low apply to Meks only.
